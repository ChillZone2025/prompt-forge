### Advertiser Feedback (from Q4 QBR meetings)

| Advertiser | Spend (Q4) | Feedback |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| TechCorp (direct) | $8,200 | "We're shifting budget to programmatic — your direct rates are 3x what we pay on the open exchange for similar audiences." |
| CloudBase (direct) | $6,400 | "CTRs on your site have dropped. Audience quality doesn't feel the same as 6 months ago." |
| Meridian Consulting (sponsor) | $5,000 | "The sponsored article didn't perform to expectations. Your readers seem to skim past sponsored labels." |
| FinanceFirst (programmatic) | $4,800 | "We buy your inventory on the open exchange at $4-5 CPM. Works for us at volume." |

*Note: Direct-sold advertising is declining because (1) programmatic is offering advertisers the same audience at lower CPMs, (2) the audience quality shift toward viral/social traffic is reducing the premium business/policy reader concentration that direct advertisers valued, and (3) no first-party data strategy exists to differentiate Meridian's audience targeting from programmatic alternatives.*
