## Reversion Risk — Approaching Sales Thresholds

### Contract Reversion Clauses

Several Hearthstone author contracts include a reversion clause triggered when annual sales fall below a specified threshold for a defined period. The standard Hearthstone reversion clause reads:

> *"If, after the fifth (5th) anniversary of publication, net sales of the Work in any consecutive twelve-month period fall below ten thousand (10,000) copies across all editions, Author may request reversion of all rights granted herein by written notice to Publisher. Publisher shall have six (6) months from receipt of such notice to either (a) demonstrate that sales have exceeded the threshold or (b) present a plan to restore the Work to active sales status. Failing both, all rights shall revert to Author."*

## Digital Rights — Perpetuity Deals (2015)

### Background

In 2015, Hearthstone's previous publisher (who retired in 2018) signed digital rights deals with ReadNow Digital, a now-defunct ebook aggregator that was later acquired by MegaBooks Corp. The deals sold **digital rights in perpetuity** for flat fees (no royalties) on 8 backlist titles.

| Title | Author | Flat Fee Paid (2015) | Current Ebook Annual Revenue (at MegaBooks) | Hearthstone Share | Revenue Lost Annually |
|-------|--------|---------------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| *Glass Meridian* | Tomoko Arai | $2,000 | $8,400 | $0 | $8,400 |
| *Bright Wound* | Tomoko Arai | $1,500 | $6,200 | $0 | $6,200 |
| *River of No Return* | James Okafor | $2,500 | $7,800 | $0 | $7,800 |
| *Overnight Frontier* | Sarah Blakemore | $1,200 | $4,600 | $0 | $4,600 |
| *The Measure of Days* | David Beaumont | $1,800 | $5,200 | $0 | $5,200 |
| *First Light* | Carlos Mendez | $800 | $2,100 | $0 | $2,100 |
| *A Trembling Ground* | Anne Whitfield | $1,000 | $1,800 | $0 | $1,800 |
| *Small Mercies* | Patricia Dunn | $900 | $1,400 | $0 | $1,400 |
| **TOTAL** | | **$11,700** | **$37,500/year** | **$0** | **$37,500/year** |

*Note: The perpetuity clause in these agreements reads: "Digital distribution rights are granted to Distributor and its successors and assigns for the full term of copyright." There is no reversion clause, no sales threshold trigger, and no termination for convenience. The only termination provisions are for material breach. MegaBooks is in full compliance with the contract terms — they are distributing the ebooks and generating revenue. Hearthstone received $11,700 total for rights that now generate $37,500 annually and are growing 12% YoY as the authors' profiles rise.*

*The authors were not consulted on these deals in 2015 (the contracts did not require author approval for digital distribution sub-licensing). However, three of the authors (Arai, Okafor, Blakemore) have since asked why their ebook sales appear so low in their royalty statements — they are seeing Amazon rankings that suggest higher sales than Hearthstone reports, because MegaBooks' sales are not reflected in Hearthstone's royalty calculations since Hearthstone receives no revenue from them.*
