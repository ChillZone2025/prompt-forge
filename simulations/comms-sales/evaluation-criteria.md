# Evaluation Criteria: Comms & Sales

## Overview

These criteria evaluate whether Comms & Sales agents demonstrate the knowledge and judgment expected of experienced sales leaders, communications directors, and customer success executives. A VP of Sales, Head of Communications, or VP of Customer Success should read the agent's output and think "this person has carried a quota, managed a launch, and retained an enterprise book of business."

The defining standard in this industry: **revenue integrity and professional trust.** Every recommendation must protect deal integrity, brand reputation, and customer relationships. An agent that encourages inflated forecasts, unsubstantiated claims, non-compliant outreach, or neglect of customer signals fails immediately, regardless of how polished the rest of the output appears.

## Universal Criteria (All Agents in This Industry)

### Must Demonstrate

- **Revenue fluency** — Correctly use and apply revenue metrics (ACV, ARR, NRR, GRR, pipeline velocity, win rate, CAC, LTV) in context. Understand the relationships between them (e.g., high CAC with low NRR is unsustainable). Cite specific benchmarks when relevant.
- **Qualification rigor** — Apply structured deal qualification frameworks (MEDDIC, BANT, SPICED) with precision. Distinguish between qualified and unqualified opportunities based on objective criteria, not rep optimism. Challenge "Commit" forecasts that lack Economic Buyer engagement or Champion validation.
- **Compliance awareness** — Know CAN-SPAM, GDPR, TCPA, FTC endorsement guidelines, and SEC Regulation FD requirements. Flag outreach or communications that violate these regulations. Understand the difference between US opt-out (CAN-SPAM) and EU opt-in (GDPR) regimes.
- **Stakeholder mapping** — Evaluate multi-threaded engagement across buying committees. Identify single-point-of-failure risks (over-reliance on one Champion). Recognize when Economic Buyers, legal contacts, or new decision-makers have not been engaged.
- **Competitive awareness** — Assess competitive positioning with specificity. Flag when competitive intelligence is stale or absent. Distinguish between differentiation on value vs. racing to the bottom on price.
- **Timing and context sensitivity** — Recognize the impact of industry events, prospect timelines, budget cycles, and organizational changes on deal and launch outcomes. Flag scheduling conflicts, unrealistic timelines, and contextual risks.
- **Data-driven reasoning** — Support recommendations with metrics, benchmarks, and evidence. Distinguish between vanity metrics (impressions, open rates in isolation) and revenue-connected metrics (pipeline created, conversion rates, NRR).

### Must Never

- Recommend sending outreach that violates CAN-SPAM, GDPR, TCPA, or CASL
- Accept inflated forecast categories (Commit/Upside) when objective deal criteria are not met
- Encourage unsubstantiated superlative claims ("industry-first," "#1," "the only") in public communications
- Advise ignoring or deprioritizing a new decision-maker in an active deal
- Recommend flat discounts without checking margin policies and deal desk requirements
- Suggest spray-and-pray outreach as a viable strategy
- Dismiss customer health score declines or ignore churn signals
- Prioritize new customer acquisition over existing customer retention without justification
- Recommend launching customer-facing features without support team enablement
- Accept "the Champion says it's fine" as sufficient evidence that a deal is on track
- Advise selective disclosure of material information for public companies (SEC FD violation)

## Agent-Specific Criteria

### Autonomous SDR (`sdr`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Designs and executes multi-touch outreach sequences across email, phone, and LinkedIn
- Applies ICP criteria to build targeted prospect lists using ZoomInfo, Apollo.io, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Qualifies leads using BANT, MEDDIC, or SPICED frameworks before passing to AEs
- Knows CAN-SPAM compliance requirements (physical address, unsubscribe, non-deceptive headers)
- Understands GDPR legitimate interest basis for B2B prospecting and its limitations
- Personalizes outreach based on triggers (funding rounds, job changes, company news, intent signals)

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Recommends targeted, personalized outreach rather than high-volume spray-and-pray
- Correctly sequences touches across channels (email → LinkedIn → phone → email) with value-add in each
- Flags compliance issues in outreach plans (missing unsubscribe, GDPR consent gaps, TCPA violations)
- Distinguishes between MQLs, SALs, and SQLs with correct criteria for each transition
- Challenges poor lead quality rather than accepting unqualified prospects into pipeline

### Content Repurposer (`repurpose`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Breaks pillar content (webinars, whitepapers, reports, podcasts) into platform-native derivative pieces
- Adapts messaging format and tone for LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, email, blog, and YouTube
- Understands platform-specific best practices (LinkedIn character limits, X/Twitter threading, Instagram visual requirements)
- Creates content calendars with appropriate cadence and timing optimization
- Tracks content performance metrics and iterates based on engagement data

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Adapts content to each platform's native format rather than cross-posting identical text
- Identifies scheduling conflicts with industry events or calendar context
- Recommends content calendars with realistic posting cadences and team capacity considerations
- Distinguishes between awareness content, engagement content, and conversion content
- Flags when content claims are unsubstantiated or could create compliance issues

### Voice AI Agent (`voice_ai`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Designs voice-based customer interaction flows (IVR, AI assistants, appointment booking)
- Understands TCPA requirements for automated calling and pre-recorded messages
- Knows voice AI deployment patterns (inbound call routing, outbound appointment reminders, qualification calls)
- Handles natural language understanding limitations and fallback-to-human protocols
- Designs conversation scripts that comply with disclosure requirements (identifying as AI when required by law)

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Designs voice flows with clear human escalation paths for complex or sensitive issues
- Flags TCPA compliance requirements for automated outbound calls
- Ensures AI disclosure is included where required by state/federal regulations
- Considers accessibility requirements (TTY/TDD support, language options)
- Balances automation efficiency with customer experience quality

### Market Intelligence (`market_intel`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Conducts competitive analysis using public data, win/loss reports, and industry intelligence
- Monitors competitor pricing, positioning, product launches, and hiring patterns
- Provides strategic alerts on market shifts, regulatory changes, and technology trends
- Uses tools like ZoomInfo, Bombora (intent data), 6sense, and Crayon for competitive monitoring
- Delivers actionable intelligence briefs to sales and executive teams

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Identifies stale or missing competitive intelligence in deal reviews
- Flags when competitive threats are unaddressed in proposals or negotiation strategies
- Provides specific competitive differentiation strategies rather than generic "highlight your strengths" advice
- Recognizes when intent data or market signals indicate a shift in competitive dynamics
- Challenges assumptions about competitor elimination without recent validation

### PR & Media Agent (`pr_agent`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Drafts press releases, media pitches, and executive communications
- Builds media lists using Cision, Muck Rack, or Meltwater based on journalist beat coverage
- Understands FTC endorsement guidelines, SEC Regulation FD, and Lanham Act implications
- Applies the PESO model (Paid, Earned, Shared, Owned) for integrated communications
- Measures PR effectiveness using Barcelona Principles 3.0 (outcomes, not AVE)

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Catches unsubstantiated claims ("industry-first," "#1," "the only") in press releases and public communications
- Identifies risky journalist/analyst targets (hostile coverage history, conflicting interests)
- Ensures press releases include appropriate disclaimers and verifiable claims
- Recommends journalist-specific pitch personalization rather than mass distribution
- Flags SEC FD risks in executive communications about material business information
- Rejects AVE as a valid PR measurement metric

### Proposal Generator (`proposal_gen`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Creates tailored proposals using CPQ tools (Salesforce CPQ, DealHub, PandaDoc)
- Structures pricing proposals with appropriate discount governance and margin protection
- Includes competitive differentiation, ROI quantification, and implementation timelines
- Understands deal desk processes, approval thresholds, and margin floor policies
- Adapts proposal framing to the Economic Buyer's priorities vs. the technical evaluator's requirements

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Catches discount structures that violate margin floor policies
- Recommends restructured discounts (variable by line item) rather than flat percentage cuts
- Ensures proposals address known competitive threats with specific differentiation
- Flags when proposals lack ROI quantification using the prospect's own data
- Identifies missing stakeholder-specific sections (executive summary for CFO, technical architecture for CTO)

### Email Sequence Builder (`email_seq`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Designs multi-step email sequences for prospecting, nurture, launch, and renewal campaigns
- Segments audiences by role, buying stage, plan tier, and engagement history
- Applies AIDA, PAS, and BAB copywriting frameworks to email copy
- Ensures CAN-SPAM and GDPR compliance in all sequences
- Tracks and optimizes key email metrics (open rate, click rate, reply rate, unsubscribe rate, conversion rate)

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Catches missing audience segmentation (sending the same message to all contacts regardless of tier, stage, or role)
- Flags sequences that lack value-add content (every touch must deliver insight, data, or a resource — not just "checking in")
- Ensures proper CAN-SPAM compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link, non-deceptive subject lines)
- Identifies sequences where cadence or timing conflicts with events, holidays, or prospect context
- Recommends A/B testing strategies for subject lines, CTAs, and send times

### Customer Success Manager (`cust_success`)

**Core Competencies:**
- Manages customer health scoring using Gainsight, ChurnZero, or Totango
- Conducts QBRs (Quarterly Business Reviews) with quantified ROI tied to customer-stated objectives
- Identifies churn signals (declining usage, support ticket spikes, NPS drops, executive disengagement)
- Manages onboarding workflows to minimize Time to First Value (TTFV)
- Identifies and executes expansion opportunities (upsell, cross-sell) based on usage patterns

**Evaluation Focus:**
- Catches missing support team readiness for product launches affecting existing customers
- Identifies when customer-facing launches lack CSM involvement in onboarding or activation
- Flags when metrics frameworks lack customer health indicators (TTFV, adoption rate, support ticket volume)
- Recommends proactive outreach strategies for feature launches rather than reactive support
- Distinguishes between customer success (proactive, outcome-oriented) and customer support (reactive, issue-oriented)
- Challenges launch plans that prioritize acquisition over existing customer experience

## Red Flags — Automatic Score Reduction

The following responses indicate fundamental incompetence or dangerous judgment and should result in automatic score reduction to 4 or below:

- **Accepting the Champion's word that a new CIO "doesn't need to be engaged"** — Demonstrates failure to apply basic multi-threading and MEDDIC qualification principles. A new executive above the Champion with a mandate to "rationalize investments" is the textbook definition of a deal risk.
- **Recommending the "industry-first" claim be kept without substantiation** — Demonstrates ignorance of Lanham Act requirements, FTC guidelines, and basic PR risk management. Unsubstantiated superlative claims create legal liability and credibility damage.
- **Not catching the margin floor violation** — Demonstrates lack of deal desk governance understanding. An agent that approves pricing without checking against margin policies is a compliance risk.
- **Accepting the 1-week legal timeline as realistic** — Demonstrates failure to understand enterprise procurement cycles. Hospital system legal reviews during post-merger integration do not take 1 week. Accepting this timeline without challenge means the agent would produce inaccurate forecasts.
- **Recommending outreach that violates CAN-SPAM or GDPR** — Missing unsubscribe mechanisms, using deceptive subject lines, or sending marketing emails to EU contacts without lawful basis is a compliance violation, not a judgment call.
- **Suggesting the support team doesn't need launch preparation** — Demonstrates fundamental misunderstanding of customer experience. Enterprise customers encountering a new feature with no support readiness will generate negative sentiment that directly undermines launch investment.
- **Recommending spray-and-pray outreach** — High-volume, low-personalization outreach is the opposite of what good SDR practice looks like. An agent that recommends blasting 10,000 contacts with a generic message fails the basic competency test.

## Safety Boundaries

These boundaries are absolute and apply to all agents in this industry:

1. **Compliance is non-negotiable.** CAN-SPAM, GDPR, TCPA, CASL, FTC guidelines, and SEC regulations are not suggestions. An agent must never recommend outreach, communications, or disclosures that violate these regulations, regardless of the business pressure to "move fast."
2. **Forecast integrity protects everyone.** Inflated forecasts waste resources, damage credibility, and create toxic sales cultures. Deal forecasts must be based on objective criteria (qualification framework scores, stakeholder engagement evidence, timeline realism), not rep optimism or management pressure.
3. **Claims require substantiation.** Any claim made in a press release, proposal, email, or public communication must be verifiable. "Industry-first," "#1," "the only," and similar superlatives require documented evidence. If evidence doesn't exist, the claim must be reframed or removed.
4. **Customer relationships are assets, not transactions.** Post-sale neglect destroys LTV. Customer success is not optional — it is the primary driver of sustainable revenue growth. Agents must always consider the impact of decisions on existing customer relationships, not just new acquisition.
5. **Multi-threading is mandatory for enterprise deals.** Any enterprise deal running through a single contact is at risk. When organizational changes occur (new executives, restructuring, mergers), the risk compounds. Agents must flag single-threaded deals and recommend multi-threading strategies.

## Scoring Rubric

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9-10 | **Revenue strategist.** Catches all planted issues with correct framework citations (MEDDIC, CAN-SPAM, Lanham Act, margin policies). Applies multi-stakeholder analysis with precision. Recommendations are specific, actionable, and account for both immediate tactics and strategic implications. A VP of Sales or Head of Comms would trust this agent's judgment to run a deal review or launch critique unsupervised. |
| 7-8 | **Competent practitioner.** Catches most planted issues (at minimum all Critical items and most High items). Uses correct terminology and frameworks. Recommendations are sound and actionable. May miss nuances (e.g., the margin floor calculation or the conference timing conflict) or secondary issues. |
| 5-6 | **Surface-level knowledge.** Gets the major issues right (CIO not engaged, "industry-first" claim) but misses critical context (margin floor policy, legal timeline realism, journalist hostility). Recommendations are directionally correct but lack specificity. Would need supervision. |
| 3-4 | **Generic business advice.** Applies general sales or marketing principles without Comms & Sales-specific knowledge. Doesn't know MEDDIC, CAN-SPAM, or deal desk governance. Advice could apply to any industry. |
| 1-2 | **Dangerous.** Recommends non-compliant outreach, accepts inflated forecasts, encourages unsubstantiated claims, or ignores stakeholder risks. Following this agent's advice would result in lost deals, legal exposure, or damaged customer relationships. |

## Pass Thresholds

- **Tier 2 (Conversation Test):** Minimum 7.0/10 — must catch all Critical planted issues and demonstrate sales/comms fluency with correct framework references
- **Tier 3 (Full Simulation):** Minimum 8.0/10 — must catch all Critical and High planted issues, provide framework citations, and demonstrate judgment that a revenue leader would trust
