## Standard Professional Approach: RICE + Impact Mapping + Roadmap Sequencing

Here's the industry-standard methodology used by leading SaaS companies like Intercom, Atlassian, and HubSpot:

### **Phase 1: Impact Mapping (Strategic Foundation)**
**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks
- **Business Objective Alignment:** Connect features to OKRs/North Star metrics
- **User Journey Mapping:** Identify friction points and opportunity areas
- **Outcome Definition:** Define measurable user behaviors and business metrics
- **Hypothesis Formation:** "We believe [feature] will drive [outcome] for [user segment]"

### **Phase 2: RICE Scoring Framework**
**Timeline:** 3-5 days per feature set

**REACH:** Users impacted per time period
- Monthly Active Users affected
- % of user base or absolute numbers
- Time-bounded (monthly/quarterly)

**IMPACT:** Improvement per user
- Scale: 3 (massive), 2 (high), 1 (medium), 0.5 (low), 0.25 (minimal)
- Based on conversion lift, engagement increase, or churn reduction

**CONFIDENCE:** Evidence certainty
- Scale: 100% (strong evidence), 80% (medium), 50% (low evidence)
- User research, A/B tests, competitive analysis, expert judgment

**EFFORT:** Development cost in "person-months"
- Engineering, design, PM, QA time
- Include technical debt and maintenance

**Formula:** RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

### **Phase 3: Roadmap Sequencing**
**Dependencies & Constraints:**
- Technical dependencies (platform requirements)
- Resource availability (team capacity)
- Market timing (competitive response, seasonality)
- Learning sequences (validation before scaling)

**Standard Sequencing Principles:**
1. **Foundation First:** Platform/infrastructure enablers
2. **Quick Wins:** High RICE, low effort for momentum
3. **Strategic Bets:** High impact features aligned to annual goals
4. **Validation Ladder:** Hypothesis → MVP → Scale

### **Phase 4: Stakeholder Alignment**
**Executive Summary Format:**
- Top 5 features with RICE scores
- Strategic rationale linking to business objectives
- Resource requirements and timeline
- Success metrics and review checkpoints

### **Industry Tools & Cadence**
- **Quarterly planning cycles** with monthly reviews
- **Tools:** ProductPlan, Aha!, Notion for documentation
- **Data sources:** Amplitude/Mixpanel for user metrics
- **Review cadence:** Weekly team, monthly stakeholder, quarterly strategic
