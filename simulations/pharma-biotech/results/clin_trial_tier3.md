# Clinical Trial Development: Standard Process & Deliverables Guide

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The clinical trial development process follows a structured pathway from protocol conception through site activation, with each phase producing specific ICH-GCP compliant deliverables. This process typically spans 6-12 months depending on therapeutic area complexity and regulatory requirements.

- **Investigator's Brochure** updates

- **Audit trail** specifications for electronic systems

- **Safety reporting** procedures (ICH E2A guidelines)

- **IRB/EC** submission packages
