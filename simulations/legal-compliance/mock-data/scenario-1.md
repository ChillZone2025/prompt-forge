## MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

**This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement")** is entered into as of January 15, 2027 ("Effective Date"), by and between NovaBridge Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation with principal offices at 1200 Innovation Drive, Suite 400, Wilmington, DE 19801 ("Vendor"), and Meridian Health Systems, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation with principal offices at 450 Medical Center Boulevard, Philadelphia, PA 19103 ("Customer").

---

### Section 1: Definitions

1.1 **"Authorized Users"** means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents who are authorized to access the Services under Customer's subscription.

1.2 **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including business plans, technical data, product plans, customer data, and financial information.

1.3 **"Customer Data"** means all data, content, and information submitted to or processed by the Services by Customer or its Authorized Users.

1.4 **"Services"** means the cloud-based workforce management software platform and related professional services described in Exhibit A.

1.5 **"Documentation"** means the user guides, online help, and other technical documentation provided by Vendor for the Services.

1.6 **"Intellectual Property Rights"** means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and other proprietary rights.

---

### Section 2: Services and Access

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement and payment of all applicable Fees, Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Services during the Term for Customer's internal business operations.

2.2 Customer shall not: (a) sublicense, sell, or transfer the Services; (b) reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Services; (c) use the Services to develop a competing product; (d) exceed the licensed number of Authorized Users.

2.3 Vendor reserves the right to modify the Services at any time, provided that such modifications do not materially reduce the functionality described in Exhibit A. Vendor shall provide 30 days notice of material changes.

---

### Section 3: Term and Termination

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months ("Initial Term").

3.2 **Auto-Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods ("Renewal Terms") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) calendar days prior to the end of the then-current term. Such notice must be delivered via email to the other party's designated contract administrator at the email address on file.

3.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within forty-five (45) days of receiving written notice of the breach.

3.4 **Termination for Convenience — Vendor.** Vendor may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to Customer. In such event, Vendor shall refund any pre-paid Fees for the unused portion of the then-current Term.

3.5 **Termination for Convenience — Customer.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days prior written notice to Vendor, subject to payment of an early termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the remaining Fees due for the then-current Term.

3.6 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination or expiration: (a) Customer's access to the Services will cease; (b) Customer shall pay all Fees incurred through the termination date; (c) Vendor shall make Customer Data available for export for a period of thirty (30) days following termination, after which Vendor may delete Customer Data; (d) Sections 5 (Fees — to the extent of amounts owed), 7 (Intellectual Property), 8 (Confidentiality), 9 (Indemnification), 10 (Limitation of Liability), 12 (Governing Law), and 13 (Dispute Resolution) shall survive termination.

---

### Section 4: Service Level Agreement

4.1 **Uptime Commitment.** Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain the Services with an availability of at least 99.5% during each calendar month, measured as: (Total Minutes in Month - Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month x 100. Scheduled maintenance windows (Saturdays 2:00 AM - 6:00 AM ET) are excluded from availability calculations.

4.2 **Service Credits.** If the Services fail to meet the availability commitment in any calendar month, Customer shall be eligible for service credits as follows:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit (% of Monthly Fee) |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 99.0% — 99.49% | 5% |
| 98.0% — 98.99% | 7.5% |
| Below 98.0% | 10% |

4.3 **Credit Cap.** Total service credits in any calendar month shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the monthly Fee for that month.

4.4 **Claim Process.** To receive service credits, Customer must submit a written request within thirty (30) days of the end of the month in which the downtime occurred. Vendor shall review and respond within fifteen (15) business days.

4.5 **Sole Remedy.** The service credits set forth in this Section 4 are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure by Vendor to meet the availability commitment.

---

### Section 5: Fees and Payment

5.1 Customer shall pay Vendor the Fees set forth in Exhibit A. Fees are invoiced monthly in advance and are due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

5.2 Fees are subject to an annual increase of up to five percent (5%) upon each Renewal Term, with notice provided at least sixty (60) days prior to the start of the Renewal Term.

5.3 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

5.4 If Customer disputes any invoice, Customer must provide written notice of the dispute within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date. Failure to dispute within this period constitutes acceptance of the invoice.

---

### Section 6: Data Security

6.1 Vendor shall implement and maintain commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6.2 Vendor shall promptly notify Customer of any confirmed Security Incident affecting Customer Data. For purposes of this section, "promptly" means within a commercially reasonable timeframe, not to exceed seventy-two (72) hours after Vendor becomes aware of a confirmed Security Incident.

6.3 Vendor shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification or equivalent third-party audit report and shall make such reports available to Customer upon request.

6.4 Vendor shall not access Customer Data except as necessary to provide the Services, perform maintenance, or comply with applicable law.

---

### Section 7: Intellectual Property

7.1 **Vendor IP.** Vendor retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Services, Documentation, and all related Intellectual Property Rights. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any Vendor Intellectual Property Rights to Customer.

7.2 **Customer Data.** Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data.

7.3 **Aggregated Data.** Notwithstanding Section 7.2, Vendor may collect and use aggregated, anonymized data derived from Customer's use of the Services ("Aggregated Data") for Vendor's business purposes, including improving the Services, benchmarking, and developing new products. Vendor shall own all right, title, and interest in Aggregated Data.

7.4 **Feedback.** If Customer provides suggestions, enhancement requests, or other feedback regarding the Services ("Feedback"), Vendor shall own all rights to such Feedback and may use it without restriction or obligation to Customer.

---

### Section 8: Confidentiality

8.1 Each party agrees to hold the other party's Confidential Information in confidence and not to disclose it to any third party except to employees, contractors, and advisors who need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.

8.2 Confidentiality obligations shall survive for three (3) years following disclosure, except for trade secrets, which shall remain confidential for as long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

8.3 Each party shall be responsible for any breach of this Section 8 by its employees, contractors, or advisors.

---

### Section 9: Indemnification

9.1 **Vendor Indemnification.** Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer and its officers, directors, employees, and agents ("Customer Indemnitees") from and against any third-party claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising from: (a) Vendor's infringement or misappropriation of a third party's Intellectual Property Rights through Customer's permitted use of the Services.

9.2 **Vendor's Obligations Under Section 9.1.** Vendor's indemnification obligation under Section 9.1 shall not apply if the infringement arises from: (a) Customer's modification of the Services; (b) Customer's combination of the Services with products not provided by Vendor; (c) Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or the Documentation.

9.3 **Customer Indemnification.** Customer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Vendor and its officers, directors, employees, and agents ("Vendor Indemnitees") from and against any third-party claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising from: (a) Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement; (b) Customer Data or any content submitted by Customer through the Services; (c) Customer's violation of any applicable law, rule, or regulation; (d) any claim by Customer's employees, contractors, or Authorized Users related to their use of the Services; (e) Customer's negligence or willful misconduct in connection with this Agreement.

9.4 **Indemnification Procedures.** The indemnified party shall: (a) promptly notify the indemnifying party of any claim; (b) grant the indemnifying party sole control of the defense and settlement; (c) cooperate with the indemnifying party at the indemnifying party's expense.

---

### Section 10: Limitation of Liability

10.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 9, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA, OR BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY, REGARDLESS OF THE CAUSE OF ACTION OR THEORY OF LIABILITY.

10.2 **Liability Cap.** EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 9, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTH PERIOD PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

10.3 **Exclusion for Willful Misconduct.** The limitations in Sections 10.1 and 10.2 shall not apply to damages arising from a party's willful misconduct.

### Section 13: Dispute Resolution

13.1 **Negotiation.** The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through negotiation between their designated representatives.

13.2 **Arbitration.** If the parties are unable to resolve a dispute within thirty (30) days of the initiation of negotiations, the dispute shall be submitted to binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association in Wilmington, Delaware. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator. The arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding.

13.3 **Injunctive Relief.** Notwithstanding Section 13.2, either party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief from any court of competent jurisdiction to protect its Intellectual Property Rights or Confidential Information.

14.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, proposals, and representations.

14.2 **Amendment.** This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both parties.

14.3 **Waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver of future enforcement.

14.4 **Severability.** If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.

14.5 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except that either party may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.

14.6 **Notices.** All notices shall be delivered to the email address of the designated contract administrator on file with the other party.

## DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA")** supplements the Master Service Agreement between NovaBridge Solutions, Inc. ("Processor") and Meridian Health Systems, Inc. ("Controller") and is effective as of the Effective Date of the Agreement.

### 1. Scope and Purpose

1.1 This DPA applies to the processing of Personal Data by Processor on behalf of Controller in connection with the Services.

1.2 **Categories of Data Subjects:** Controller's employees, contractors, and temporary staff.

1.3 **Types of Personal Data:** Names, contact information, employee identification numbers, work schedules, time records, credentials and certifications, emergency contact information, and to the extent uploaded by Controller, Social Security numbers and date of birth.

1.4 **Processing Purpose:** Providing the Services as described in the Agreement and Exhibit A.

### 2. Controller Obligations

2.1 Controller is responsible for ensuring it has a lawful basis for transferring Personal Data to Processor.

2.2 Controller shall inform Processor of any specific data handling requirements under applicable law.

### 3. Processor Obligations

3.1 Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller, unless required by applicable law.

3.2 Processor shall ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality.

3.3 Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

3.4 Processor shall not engage a sub-processor without prior written authorization from Controller. Processor's current list of sub-processors is available at [www.novabridge.io/subprocessors].

3.5 Processor shall assist Controller in responding to data subject requests to the extent technically feasible.

### 4. International Data Transfers

4.1 Processor may transfer Personal Data to countries outside the European Economic Area ("EEA") as necessary to provide the Services. For any such transfers, Processor shall ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for international data transfers.

4.2 The applicable Standard Contractual Clauses are incorporated by reference and attached hereto as Annex B.

**[NOTE: Annex B — Standard Contractual Clauses — is referenced but not included in this document package.]**

### 5. Data Breach Notification

5.1 Processor shall notify Controller of any Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of such breach.

5.2 The notification shall include: (a) nature of the breach; (b) categories and approximate number of data subjects affected; (c) likely consequences; (d) measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1 Upon termination of the Agreement, Processor shall, at Controller's election, return or delete all Personal Data within thirty (30) days, unless retention is required by applicable law.

### 7. Audit Rights

7.1 Processor shall make available to Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Controller or an authorized auditor.

7.2 Audits shall be limited to once per twelve (12) month period, with at least thirty (30) days prior written notice, and shall be conducted during normal business hours.
