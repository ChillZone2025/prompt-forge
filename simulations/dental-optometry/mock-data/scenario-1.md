## PLANTED ISSUES (for evaluation — agent should catch these)

1. **Collections rate is 89.1% vs. 98% benchmark — $62,735 gap driven by insurance aging.** Insurance AR over 90 days is 28.2% of total AR ($59,700) vs. the 5% benchmark. The root cause is clear: no dedicated insurance coordinator, claims over 90 days haven't been worked in 3 months, and the Dentrix aging report hasn't been reviewed since December 2025. The front desk team is trying to do check-in/check-out AND claims follow-up, and the claims are losing. The $59,700 in aged claims is likely 40-60% recoverable if worked promptly, with older claims approaching timely filing deadlines (most payers: 90-180 days from date of service or denial). The practice needs a dedicated insurance coordinator immediately — at even $22/hour, this role pays for itself many times over.

2. **Treatment plan acceptance at 42% vs. 85% benchmark — case presentation process is fundamentally broken.** The audit reveals the causes: no intraoral camera usage (14% of exams), no treatment coordinator involvement (0%), doctors presenting cost in the operatory (100%), no financial options presented proactively, no same-day scheduling protocol, and zero follow-up on unscheduled treatment. The $306,720 in unscheduled treatment value represents massive revenue leakage. CareCredit and Sunbit terminals sit unused unless patients specifically ask. This is a training and workflow problem — the practice needs a dedicated treatment coordinator role, standardized case presentation protocol, mandatory intraoral camera usage, and a follow-up system for unscheduled treatment.

3. **Hygiene department producing at 10.3% of total production vs. 30-33% benchmark — perio underdiagnosis is the root cause.** With a perio diagnosis rate of 8% vs. the epidemiological expected rate of ~47%, the hygienists are performing prophylaxis on patients who should be receiving SRP and periodontal maintenance. This is both a clinical standard-of-care failure (performing prophy on perio patients is undertreatment) and a financial failure (SRP reimburses at $300/quadrant vs. $120/prophy). The hygiene production gap is $150,250/month. The fix requires: calibrated perio protocols (full-mouth probing every patient, every visit), clear diagnostic criteria for perio vs. prophy classification, hygienist training on perio diagnosis standards, and doctor buy-in on supporting hygienists' perio diagnoses.

4. **Dr. Thompson's production is 44% below GP peer average — 25% open chair time is the primary driver.** The schedule analysis shows consistent open slots across every day of the week, compounded by no same-day fill protocol, no schedule templating, no short-call list, and no morning huddle review of open time. At $44,000/month in lost production ($528,000 annualized), this is the largest single-provider opportunity. The fix is operational: implement schedule templating, maintain and work a short-call list, build same-day fill protocols for cancellations, and include Dr. Thompson's schedule in morning huddle for daily production goal-setting.

5. **New patient no-show rate is 35% — no confirmation protocol exists beyond a single text.** The practice sends one automated text the day before and nothing else. No 7-day welcome sequence, no 3-day reminder, no day-of confirmation, no no-show follow-up. Weave's full suite is contracted but not configured. At $8,470/month in lost first-visit production ($101,640 annualized) plus the incalculable lifetime value loss, this is an urgent fix. Best practice: 7-day welcome text/email with office tour video, 3-day reminder with driving directions and forms link, 1-day confirmation requiring response, day-of "we're excited to see you" text, and immediate no-show follow-up call within 1 hour.

6. **Recall reactivation rate is 0% — 812 patients overdue with zero outreach.** The practice has 812 patients who've fallen out of the recall cycle, and not a single one has been contacted. Lighthouse 360 was purchased for this purpose but never configured beyond basic reminders. The estimated recoverable value is $81,200/year (25% reactivation rate × 812 patients × $400 annual value). The fix: configure Lighthouse 360 reactivation campaigns immediately (multi-touch sequence: text → email → phone call → direct mail), segment by time overdue (18-24 months are the warmest leads), and assign a team member to work the reactivation phone list daily.
