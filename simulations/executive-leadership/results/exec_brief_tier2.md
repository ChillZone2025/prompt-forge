## EXECUTIVE COMMUNICATION STANDARDS: One-Pagers, Situation Summaries & Talking Points

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
Professional executive communications follow the BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) methodology with structured hierarchies designed for rapid decision-making. C-suite audiences expect immediate clarity on impact, options, and required actions within scanning timeframes of 60-90 seconds.

---

## INDUSTRY STANDARD FRAMEWORKS

**ONE-PAGERS (250-300 words)**
- **BLUF Header:** 2-3 sentence executive summary in highlighted box
- **Structure:** Situation → Impact → Options → Recommendation
- **Visual Hierarchy:** Bold headers, bullet points, white space optimization
- **Decision Focus:** Clear next steps with owner and timeline
- **McKinsey Standard:** "If you only read one paragraph, make it the first one"

**SITUATION SUMMARIES (400-500 words)**
- **SOAR Framework:** Situation, Options, Analysis, Recommendation
- **Timeline Integration:** Key milestones and decision points mapped
- **Stakeholder Impact Matrix:** Primary constituencies affected
- **Risk Assessment:** High/Medium/Low classifications with mitigation
- **Goldman Sachs Approach:** "Tell them what you're going to tell them, tell them, then tell them what you told them"

**TALKING POINTS (Modular Format)**
- **Key Messages:** 3-5 core points with 30-second elevator versions
- **Supporting Data:** Metrics and proof points for each message
- **Q&A Preparation:** Anticipated questions with approved responses
- **Bridging Statements:** Techniques to redirect difficult questions
- **BCG Standard:** Each point must pass the "So what?" test

## CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

**Cognitive Load Management:** Executive attention spans average 7-11 minutes for complex topics
**Pyramid Principle:** Lead with conclusions, support with reasoning
**MECE Structure:** Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive information architecture
**Action Orientation:** Every briefing must drive to specific, time-bound decisions
