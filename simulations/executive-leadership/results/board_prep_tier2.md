## **KPI NARRATIVE METHODOLOGY**

**"Variance-Driver-Implication" Format:**
- **What happened:** Quantify the metric change vs. expectations
- **Why it happened:** Root cause analysis with specific drivers
- **What it means:** Strategic implications and required actions

**Example:** "Customer acquisition cost increased 15% vs. plan ($142 vs. $124), driven by iOS 14.5 privacy changes reducing Facebook ad effectiveness.
