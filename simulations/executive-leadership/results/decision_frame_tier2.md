## Standard Professional Approach: Executive Options Analysis Framework

For executive and leadership decision-making, the standard professional approach follows this **four-phase methodology**:

### **Phase 1: Decision Architecture (MECE Framework)**
- **Options Generation:** Brainstorm using mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive categories
- **Criteria Definition:** Weight factors like Strategic Fit (30%), Financial Impact (25%), Risk Profile (20%), Implementation Feasibility (15%), Timeline (10%)
- **Stakeholder Mapping:** RACI matrix identifying decision-makers, influencers, and impacted parties

### **Phase 2: Quantitative Analysis**
- **Multi-Criteria Decision Matrix:** Score each option 1-5 against weighted criteria
- **Financial Modeling:** NPV, IRR, payback period with 3-scenario modeling (optimistic/realistic/pessimistic)
- **Expected Value Calculation:** Probability-weighted outcomes for each option

### **Phase 3: Risk Assessment**
- **5x5 Risk Matrix:** Plot probability (1-5) vs. impact (1-5) for each option
- **Risk Register:** Identify, assess, and plan mitigation strategies
- **Sensitivity Analysis:** Test key assumptions and break-even points

### **Phase 4: Executive Recommendation**
- **Traffic Light Dashboard:** Green/Yellow/Red recommendation with confidence interval
- **Business Case Summary:** One-page executive brief with decision rationale
- **Implementation Roadmap:** 30-60-90 day milestones and success metrics

**Industry Standard Tools:** McKinsey decision trees, BCG portfolio matrices, and Monte Carlo simulations for high-stakes choices.
