In the A&E industry, these three functions form the backbone of comprehensive project cost management. Here's the established professional framework:

### **QUANTITY TAKEOFFS**

**Industry Standards:**
- **CSI MasterFormat Organization:** Structure takeoffs by 50 divisions (00-49) for consistency across project teams
- **UNIFORMAT II Classification:** Use elemental groupings (A-G systems) for early design phase estimates

**Professional Methodology:**
1. **Systematic Approach:** Work through drawings in logical sequence (sitework → structure → envelope → interiors → MEP)
2. **Accuracy Levels:** Target ±15% for schematic design, ±10% for design development, ±5% for construction documents
3. **Documentation:** Maintain detailed measurement logs with drawing references and calculation methods
4. **Quality Control:** Implement peer review and cross-checking protocols

### **UNIT PRICING**

**Established Sources:**
- **RSMeans Construction Cost Data:** Industry standard for labor, material, and equipment costs
- **Local Market Data:** Subcontractor quotes, supplier pricing, historical project costs
- **Regional Adjustments:** ENR Construction Cost Index, local wage rates, material availability

**Pricing Structure:**
- **Direct Costs:** Labor (including benefits/taxes), materials, equipment, subcontracts
- **Indirect Costs:** General conditions (10-15%), overhead (5-10%), profit (5-10%)
- **Market Factors:** Location multipliers, seasonal adjustments, escalation rates

- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS):** Align with project delivery method and contract packaging
