## **SUBMITTAL PROCESS (Design & Construction Phase)**

**Shop Drawings & Product Data:**
- Follow specification section requirements and submittal schedule
- Use standard transmittal forms (often AIA G810 or similar)
- Include: Cover sheet, specification compliance matrix, product cut sheets, calculations, and certifications
- Status tracking: Submitted → Under Review → Approved/Approved as Noted/Rejected/Revise & Resubmit

**Professional Certifications:**
- PE stamp requirements for structural, MEP calculations
- Manufacturer certifications and warranties
- Code compliance documentation
- Testing and inspection reports

## **CLARIFICATION REQUESTS (Pre-Bid)**

**Standard Protocol:**
- Submit through designated portal (often project website or email)
- Include: Project name, specification section reference, page number, specific question
- Request format: "Please clarify [specific requirement] in Section [X.X.X]"
- Typical deadline: 7-10 days before bid due date

**Common A&E Clarification Topics:**
- Conflicting dimensions between drawings and specifications
- Material specification ambiguities
- Code interpretation questions
- Scope boundary clarifications between trades

## **BID RESPONSE STRUCTURE (Qualifications-Based Selection)**

**SF 330 (Federal) or Equivalent State Forms:**
