# A&E Procurement Process & Deliverable Guide

I'll walk you through the three distinct phases of A&E procurement documentation, each with specific deliverables and timelines.

## PHASE 1: BID/PROPOSAL RESPONSE PROCESS

### **Pre-Submission Activities (2-4 weeks before deadline)**
**Key Deliverables:**
- **Pre-bid meeting questions** - Technical clarifications on scope, codes, site conditions
- **RFI letters** - Formal requests for information on ambiguous specifications
- **Addendum acknowledgments** - Written confirmation of receipt and incorporation

**Standard Process:**
1. Solicitation analysis and compliance matrix creation
2. Go/no-go decision based on qualification criteria
3. Team assembly and responsibility assignments
4. Content development following evaluation criteria

### **Primary Submission Package**
**For RFPs (Design Projects):**
- SF-330 forms (federal) or state equivalents
- Technical proposal narrative (10-25 pages)
- Project team qualifications and CVs
- Relevant experience with project fact sheets
- Fee proposal (if requested)

**For RFQs (Qualification-Based Selection):**
- Firm qualifications and experience
- Key personnel resumes
- Past performance references
- Specialized certifications (LEED, specialty expertise)

## PHASE 2: CLARIFICATION PROCESS (Post-Submission)

### **Types of Clarifications You May Encounter:**
**Technical Clarifications:**
- Scope interpretation requests
- Code compliance questions  
- Design criteria verification
- Performance specification details

**Administrative Clarifications:**
- Team member availability confirmation
- Fee breakdown requests (if interviews reached)
- Reference contact verification
- Insurance/bonding capability confirmation

### **Standard Deliverables:**
- **Clarification response letters** (24-48 hour turnaround typical)
- **Supplemental technical data** (calculations, specifications, drawings)
- **Updated project team information** (if changes occurred)
- **Interview presentation materials** (for shortlisted firms)

## PHASE 3: SUBMITTAL PROCESS (Post-Award)

### **Design Phase Submittals:**

- Initial review (5-10 business days)

- Final approval and distribution
