|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| CorePower Yoga | 0.8 miles | Yoga only | $159/month | Brand recognition, evening/weekend classes |
| Orange Theory | 1.2 miles | HIIT | $169/month | Heart rate tracking, evening classes, data-driven |
