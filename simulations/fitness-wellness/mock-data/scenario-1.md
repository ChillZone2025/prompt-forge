## Training Program Overview

### Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Foundation

| Day | Focus | Duration |
|-----|-------|----------|
| Monday (in-person) | Full body strength — compound lifts | 60 min |
| Tuesday (independent) | Steady-state cardio — treadmill incline walk | 40 min |
| Wednesday (in-person) | Upper body + core | 60 min |
| Thursday (independent) | Steady-state cardio — elliptical | 40 min |
| Friday (in-person) | Lower body + mobility | 60 min |

**Volume:** 12 working sets per session (strength days), RPE 6-7
**Cardio:** 2x/week, 40 min, 60-65% MHR

### Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Progression

| Day | Focus | Duration |
|-----|-------|----------|
| Monday (in-person) | Upper body push/pull — heavier loading | 60 min |
| Tuesday (independent) | HIIT — 30 sec sprint / 90 sec recovery x 10 + 20 min steady state | 50 min |
| Wednesday (in-person) | Lower body — squat/deadlift focus | 60 min |
| Thursday (independent) | HIIT — same protocol + 20 min steady state | 50 min |
| Friday (in-person) | Full body + conditioning circuit | 60 min |
| **Saturday (added Week 6)** | **Independent — 45 min fasted steady-state cardio** | **45 min** |

**Volume:** 18 working sets per session (strength days), RPE 7-8
**Cardio:** Increased from 2x to 3x/week, added HIIT, added fasted cardio Saturday

### Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): "Aggressive Push"

| Day | Focus | Duration |
|-----|-------|----------|
| Monday (in-person) | Upper body — high volume (drop sets, supersets) | 75 min |
| Tuesday (independent) | HIIT — 20 sec sprint / 40 sec recovery x 15 + 25 min steady state | 55 min |
| Wednesday (in-person) | Lower body — high volume | 75 min |
| Thursday (independent) | HIIT — same protocol + 25 min steady state | 55 min |
| Friday (in-person) | Full body metabolic conditioning | 75 min |
| Saturday (independent) | 60 min fasted steady-state cardio | 60 min |
| **Sunday (added Week 10)** | **Independent — 30 min "active recovery" (light jog + abs)** | **30 min** |

**Volume:** 24 working sets per session (strength days), RPE 8-9
**Cardio:** 4x/week (3 HIIT + 1-2 steady state), session duration increased to 55-75 min

**NOTE: No deload week was programmed in any phase.**
