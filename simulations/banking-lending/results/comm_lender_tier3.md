# Deep-Dive Analysis Framework & Prioritized Action Plan

## PRIORITY 1: FINANCIAL ANALYSIS FOUNDATION (Days 1-2)

### **Critical Financial Metrics to Calculate:**

**Cash Flow Analysis:**
- EBITDA normalization (remove one-time items, owner compensation adjustments)
- Free cash flow calculation: EBITDA - CapEx - Principal Payments - Tax Distributions
- Debt service coverage ratio: Cash Available for Debt Service ÷ Required Debt Service
- **Target:** Minimum 1.25x DSCR, preferably 1.35x+

**Leverage Assessment:**
- Senior leverage: Senior Debt ÷ TTM EBITDA
- Total leverage: Total Debt ÷ TTM EBITDA  
- **Industry benchmarks:** Most sectors target <3.0x senior, <4.0x total

**Liquidity Analysis:**
- Working capital trends and seasonal patterns
- Available revolver capacity vs. typical usage
- Cash conversion cycle (DPO + DSO - DIO)

### **Immediate Next Steps:**
1. **Spread 13 quarters of financials** using standardized format
2. **Calculate trailing twelve months (TTM) metrics** for most recent period
3. **Identify normalization adjustments** (litigation, extraordinary items, family expenses)
4. **Benchmark against industry medians** using RMA/Sageworks data

## PRIORITY 2: COVENANT STRUCTURING (Days 2-3)

### **Covenant Package Design:**

**Senior Leverage Covenant:**
- Set at 75-80% of projected worst-case performance
- Include step-downs if credit quality warrants (e.g., 3.5x Year 1, 3.25x Year 2+)
- **Testing frequency:** Quarterly for term loans, monthly for asset-based facilities

**Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio:**
- Include all mandatory debt service, lease payments, and capex requirements
- Minimum 1.15x for strong credits, 1.25x for average credits
- **Calculation:** (EBITDA - Capex - Tax Distributions) ÷ (Interest + Principal + Leases)

**Minimum Tangible Net Worth:**
- Base level = current TNW minus reasonable annual draws
- Growth requirement: 50% of annual net income (if positive)

### **Advanced Covenant Considerations:**
- **Basket provisions:** Permitted acquisitions, capex limits, additional debt
- **Equity cure rights:** Allow sponsor equity injection to cure covenant breaches
- **Testing modifications:** Exclude acquisition impacts for 2-4 quarters

### **Action Items:**
