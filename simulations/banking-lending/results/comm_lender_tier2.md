## COVENANT ANALYSIS
**Standard Methodology:**
- **Historical Performance:** 8-12 quarters of actual covenant calculations
- **Stress Testing:** Downside case modeling (typically 10-15% EBITDA decline)
- **Cushion Analysis:** Distance between projected ratios and covenant levels
- **Seasonal Adjustments:** Monthly testing for seasonal businesses

**Key Ratios by Facility Type:**
- **Cash Flow:** Max Total Leverage (3.0-5.0x), Min DSCR (1.20-1.35x)
- **Asset-Based:** Borrowing base certificates, minimum availability requirements

## CREDIT MEMOS
**Standard Format:**
1. **Executive Summary** (1 page): Recommendation, facility summary, key risks
2. **Company Overview:** Business model, market position, management assessment
3. **Financial Analysis:** Trend analysis, peer benchmarking, cash flow quality
4. **Risk Assessment:** Industry, operational, financial, structural risks
5. **Mitigating Factors:** Collateral, guarantees, covenant protection
6. **Recommendation:** Pricing rationale, covenant justification, conditions
