## Standard Professional Approach for Application Review, DTI Analysis & Disclosure Prep

### **Phase 1: Application Review (URLA Completeness)**
**Professional Protocol:**
- Verify all URLA sections 1-10 are complete with required signatures
- Cross-reference borrower disclosures against application data
- Confirm property address matches across all documents
- Validate income/employment information completeness
- Check for internal inconsistencies or red flags

**Documentation Checklist:**
- 4506-C/4506-T tax transcript authorization
- Complete asset documentation (2 months statements)
- Employment verification letters/paystubs
- Insurance declarations/flood determination
- Purchase contract or property deed (refinance)

### **Phase 2: DTI Analysis (Front-End & Back-End Ratios)**
**Standard Methodology:**
- **Front-End DTI:** (PITIA ÷ Monthly Gross Income) × 100
- **Back-End DTI:** (Total Monthly Obligations ÷ Monthly Gross Income) × 100

**Agency Guidelines:**
- Conventional: 28%/36% (standard), up to 45% with compensating factors
- FHA: 31%/43% (standard), up to 57% with automated approval
- VA: No front-end limit/41% back-end (standard)
- USDA: 29%/41% (standard)

**Income Calculation Standards:**
- W-2: 2-year average for variable income, current for stable salary
- Self-Employed: 2-year tax return average minus 2% depreciation add-back
- Include: base, overtime (2-yr average), bonus (2-yr average), rental income (75% of gross)

### **Phase 3: Disclosure Preparation (TRID Compliance)**
**Required Timeline:**
- Loan Estimate: Within 3 business days of application receipt
- Closing Disclosure: 3 business days before closing
- Intent to Proceed: Required before proceeding with verification

**Critical Elements:**
- Accurate APR calculation including all finance charges
- Proper loan program identification and terms
- Complete itemization of closing costs
- Cash-to-close calculation with all scenarios
- Regulatory notices (CHARM, servicing disclosure, etc.)

### **Quality Control Standards:**
- Triple-check all numerical calculations
- Verify investor overlay compliance beyond agency minimums  
- Document compensating factors for marginal approvals
- Flag any potential Fair Lending concerns
- Prepare comprehensive underwriter summary with risk assessment
