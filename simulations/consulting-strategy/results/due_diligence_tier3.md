# Detailed Due Diligence Analysis Framework & Action Plan

## DEEP-DIVE ANALYTICAL METHODOLOGIES

### **Financial Due Diligence - Advanced Techniques**

#### **Quality of Earnings Analysis**
**Specific Steps:**
1. **Revenue Recognition Analysis**
   - Map revenue streams to accounting standards (ASC 606)
   - Identify bill-and-hold arrangements, channel stuffing
   - Analyze contract terms for performance obligations
   - Test revenue cut-off procedures (last 5 business days of quarters)

2. **EBITDA Normalization Process**
   - Remove one-time items (restructuring, acquisition costs)
   - Normalize management fees and related-party transactions  
   - Adjust for run-rate synergies and cost savings
   - Calculate "normalized EBITDA" for valuation purposes

3. **Working Capital Deep-Dive**
   - Calculate 13-month rolling averages for DSO, DIO, DPO
   - Identify seasonal patterns and cash conversion cycles
   - Benchmark against industry standards (using RMA data)
   - Project normalized working capital requirements

**Priority Action:** Build integrated 3-statement model with quarterly granularity

#### **Advanced Valuation Framework**
**DCF Model Components:**
- Revenue build-up by business unit/geography
- EBITDA bridge analysis (volume vs. price vs. mix)
- CapEx requirements linked to revenue growth assumptions
- Terminal value using both perpetuity growth (2-3%) and exit multiple methods
- Monte Carlo sensitivity analysis on key variables

**Comparable Analysis:**
- Screen 15-20 public comps using multiple criteria (industry, size, geography, growth)
- Calculate LTM and NTM multiples (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, P/E)
- Apply haircuts for liquidity, size, and control premiums

---

### **Commercial Due Diligence - Market Intelligence**

#### **Market Sizing Methodology**
**TAM/SAM/SOM Framework:**
1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**
   - Use multiple data sources: IBISWorld, Frost & Sullivan, Gartner
   - Cross-validate with government statistics (Census Bureau)
   - Apply bottom-up and top-down approaches

2. **Serviceable Available Market (SAM)**
   - Filter by geography, customer segments, product categories
   - Account for regulatory restrictions and competitive barriers
   - Size current addressable opportunity

3. **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**
   - Historical market share analysis and trajectory
   - Competitive positioning assessment
   - Realistic market penetration scenarios (base/upside/downside)

#### **Competitive Intelligence Framework**
**Porter's Five Forces Analysis:**
- **Supplier Power:** Concentration ratios, switching costs, differentiation
- **Buyer Power:** Customer concentration, price sensitivity, alternatives
- **Competitive Rivalry:** Market share fragmentation, growth rates, differentiation
- **Threat of Substitutes:** Technology disruption, cost-performance trade-offs
- **Barriers to Entry:** Capital requirements, regulatory hurdles, network effects

**Priority Action:** Create competitive positioning map with bubble chart (market share vs. growth rate vs. profitability)
