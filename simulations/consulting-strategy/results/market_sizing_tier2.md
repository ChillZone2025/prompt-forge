## Professional TAM/SAM/SOM Bottoms-Up Methodology in Consulting & Strategy

The gold standard approach in top-tier consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) follows this **rigorous 6-step framework**:

### **1. Market Definition & Segmentation**
- Define precise market boundaries (product/service scope, geography, customer types)
- Segment customers by distinct needs, behaviors, and willingness-to-pay
- Apply Jobs-to-be-Done framework to validate market boundaries
- **Output:** Customer persona matrix with segment sizing

### **2. Bottoms-Up Unit Economics Build**
```
TAM = Σ(Segment Size × Average Revenue Per User × Penetration Rate)
```
- Count addressable customers per segment (not total population)
- Calculate realistic ARPU based on value delivery and pricing analysis
- Model adoption curves using Bass diffusion or technology lifecycle frameworks
- **Tools:** Excel/Python with Monte Carlo simulation for uncertainty

### **3. Three-Horizon Validation (Triangulation)**
- **Bottoms-up:** Customer count × unit economics
- **Top-down:** Industry reports scaled to specific use case
- **Value theory:** Economic value created/captured analysis
- Flag discrepancies >30% for deeper investigation

### **4. Competitive Constraint Modeling**
- Map competitive landscape intensity by segment
- Calculate realistic market share capture based on competitive positioning
- Apply Porter's Five Forces to validate market accessibility
- **SAM Calculation:** TAM × (1 - Competitive Constraint Factor)

### **5. Go-to-Market Reality Check**
- Model distribution channel capacity and economics
- Factor in sales cycle length, customer acquisition costs
- Include operational scaling constraints
- **SOM Calculation:** SAM × GTM Execution Probability × Time Factor

### **6. Sensitivity & Scenario Planning**
- Build best/base/worst case scenarios (typically ±50% range)
- Test sensitivity to key assumptions (customer count, pricing, adoption rate)
- Include confidence intervals (usually 70-90% confidence)
- **Delivery:** Executive summary with methodology appendix

### **Professional Standards:**
- Always state assumptions explicitly with confidence levels
- Include data source attribution and quality assessment
- Provide 3-5 year projections maximum with expanding uncertainty bands
- Present ranges, never single-point estimates

**Typical deliverable structure:** Executive summary (1 slide) → Market definition (2 slides) → Bottoms-up model (3-4 slides) → Validation & scenarios (2 slides) → Appendix with detailed assumptions.
