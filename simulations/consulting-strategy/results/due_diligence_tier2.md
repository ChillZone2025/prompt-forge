## Standard Professional Approach for Risk Assessment, Deal Analysis & Investment Memos

In the Consulting & Strategy industry, particularly for PE/VC and M&A advisory work, here's the established professional framework:

### **1. RISK ASSESSMENT METHODOLOGY**

**Risk Categorization Framework:**
- **Financial Risks**: Revenue concentration, working capital volatility, debt covenant compliance, FX exposure
- **Operational Risks**: Key person dependency, scalability constraints, supply chain vulnerabilities
- **Market Risks**: Competitive threats, regulatory changes, cyclicality, technology disruption
- **Strategic Risks**: Integration complexity, cultural fit, synergy realization timeline

**Quantitative Risk Matrix:**
- Probability scoring (1-5 scale: Remote to Certain)
- Impact assessment ($MM ranges and % EBITDA impact)
- Risk-adjusted NPV calculations using Monte Carlo simulation
- Sensitivity analysis on key value drivers (typically ±20% scenarios)

### **2. DEAL ANALYSIS STRUCTURE**

**Financial Analysis Workstreams:**
- **Quality of Earnings**: Revenue recognition, one-time items, normalized EBITDA adjustments
- **Working Capital Analysis**: 13-month average, seasonality patterns, DSO/DPO/DIO trends
- **Cash Flow Modeling**: Unlevered FCF projections, capex requirements, tax optimization
- **Leverage Analysis**: Debt capacity, covenant headroom, refinancing risks

**Commercial Due Diligence:**
- TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing with 3rd party validation
- Customer concentration analysis and retention metrics
- Competitive positioning using BCG Growth-Share Matrix
- Revenue bridge analysis (organic vs. inorganic growth)

### **3. INVESTMENT MEMO STANDARDS**

**Executive Summary (1-2 pages):**
- Investment thesis in 3-4 bullet points
- Key value drivers and risk factors
- Financial snapshot (Revenue/EBITDA/Multiple)
- Go/No-go recommendation with confidence level

**Standard Memo Structure:**
1. **Situation Overview** (company profile, transaction rationale)
2. **Investment Highlights** (3-5 key strengths)
3. **Financial Performance** (historical analysis + projections)
4. **Market Position** (competitive assessment)
5. **Key Risks** (probability-weighted impact analysis)
6. **Valuation Analysis** (multiple methodologies)
7. **Recommendation** (terms, conditions, walk-away scenarios)

**Professional Standards:**
- **Length**: 15-25 pages for full memo, 5-7 pages for IC summary
- **Benchmarking**: Include 5-7 comparable transactions
- **Scenarios**: Base/upside/downside with probability weights
- **Decision Timeline**: 30/60/90-day action items

### **4. INDUSTRY BEST PRACTICES**

**Data Validation Requirements:**
- Independent market research (Gartner, IDC, Frost & Sullivan)
- Management reference calls (3-5 former colleagues/customers)
- Expert network interviews for technical/regulatory insights
- Financial audit trail back to source documents

**Quality Control Checkpoints:**
- Cross-team review (financial, commercial, operational workstreams)
- Senior partner sign-off on key assumptions
- External advisor validation for specialized risks
- Compliance review for regulatory/legal issues
