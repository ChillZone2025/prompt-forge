- **Situation:** Current business context (1 slide)

1. **Executive Summary** (Key insight + recommendation)
2. **Supporting Pillars** (3-4 main arguments maximum)
3. **Evidence Base** (Data, analysis, benchmarks per pillar)
4. **Implementation** (Timeline, resources, success metrics)

**Standard Consulting Frameworks:**
- **BCG 2x2 matrices** for positioning and prioritization
- **Porter's Five Forces** for competitive context
- **McKinsey 7S** for organizational capability assessment
- **Value chain analysis** for operational improvement opportunities

### VISUAL EXECUTION LAYER

- **Slide 1:** Executive summary with key recommendation

- **Appendix:** Detailed analysis, financial models, risk mitigation

**Professional Formatting:**
- One key message per slide (slide titles as conclusions)
- Maximum 6 bullets per slide
- Data visualizations support narrative flow
- Action-oriented language throughout
