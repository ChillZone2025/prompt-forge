# TAM/SAM/SOM Bottoms-Up Modeling: Complete Process Guide

## METHODOLOGY OVERVIEW

**TAM/SAM/SOM Framework:**
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** Universe of all potential customers who could theoretically buy your solution
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** Portion of TAM you can realistically target given business model constraints
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** Share of SAM you can realistically capture given competitive dynamics and execution capabilities

**Bottoms-Up Approach:** Build market size from granular customer units rather than top-down industry estimates, providing greater precision and actionable insights.

## STANDARD PROCESS WORKFLOW

### Phase 1: Market Definition & Segmentation (Week 1)
**Activities:**
- Define target customer segments using firmographic, demographic, and behavioral criteria
- Establish geographic and product scope boundaries
- Create customer personas with specific use cases and pain points
- Map customer decision-making processes and buying criteria

**Key Questions:**
- Who specifically experiences the problem you solve?
- What triggers purchase decisions in this segment?
- How do customers currently solve this problem?

### Phase 2: Customer Universe Sizing (Week 1-2)
**Data Collection:**
- Census/government databases for demographic segments (B2C)
- Industry databases (Hoovers, ZoomInfo) for firmographic segments (B2B)
- Trade association reports for industry-specific counts
- Survey research for behavioral segmentation validation

**Calculation Method:**
```
TAM = (# of Target Customers) × (Average Annual Spend per Customer)
```

### Phase 3: Addressability Analysis (Week 2)
**SAM Refinement:**
- Geographic constraints (regulatory, distribution, language)
- Business model limitations (price points, service requirements)
- Customer accessibility (direct sales vs. channel conflicts)
- Regulatory/compliance restrictions

**Waterfall Analysis:**
```
SAM = TAM × Geographic Reach % × Business Model Fit % × Regulatory Compliance %
```

### Phase 4: Competitive Reality Check (Week 2-3)
**SOM Modeling:**
- Market maturity assessment (nascent, growing, mature, declining)
- Competitive landscape mapping with market share estimates
- Entry barrier analysis (switching costs, network effects, capital requirements)
- Customer acquisition assumptions based on sales capacity and marketing spend

**Penetration Assumptions:**
```
SOM = SAM × Market Penetration Rate × Competitive Win Rate
```

### Phase 5: Validation & Sensitivity Analysis (Week 3)
**Triangulation Methods:**
- Top-down industry reports comparison
- Analogous market benchmarking
- Expert interview validation
- Customer survey data correlation

**Sensitivity Testing:**
- High/medium/low scenarios for key assumptions
- Monte Carlo simulation for uncertainty ranges
- Break-even analysis for minimum viable market size
