## PLANTED ISSUES (for evaluation — agent should catch these)

1. **Revenue discrepancy:** Self-reported annual revenue ($2.4M) is more than double the most recent tax return ($1.18M). Major red flag — either inflating current revenue or prior tax fraud.

2. **DTI calculation problem:** Stated personal income $185K, but the business only nets $180K and he's the sole owner. Where is the extra $5K coming from? Also, existing debt service ($38,400) + proposed new debt (~$7,500/month = $90,000/year) = $128,400 total debt on $180K income = 71.3% DTI. Well above standard 43-50% thresholds.

3. **Undisclosed debt:** The $95,000 equipment loan at another bank was NOT listed in the application. This is a material omission that affects total leverage and DTI.

4. **Existing blanket lien:** First Capital Leasing has a UCC blanket lien on all business assets. The offered inventory collateral is already encumbered. The bank would be in a subordinate position.

5. **Collateral shortfall:** $120K inventory against a $350K loan = 34% LTV on collateral alone. Combined with the blanket lien, effective collateral coverage is near zero.
