### Attribution Note

All revenue attribution in this report uses last-touch (last non-direct click) attribution. Under this model, the final marketing touchpoint before conversion receives 100% of credit. Multi-touch analysis is not currently implemented.

Example customer journey (anonymized, typical enterprise deal):

1. **Day 1** — Clicked LinkedIn Thought Leadership post → visited blog → read case study → left
2. **Day 14** — Googled "professional services automation" → clicked organic result → read buyer's guide → left
3. **Day 28** — Received email nurture (case study follow-up) → clicked through → requested demo

Under last-touch attribution, this conversion is attributed 100% to **Email Marketing**. LinkedIn (awareness) and Organic Search (consideration) receive zero credit.
