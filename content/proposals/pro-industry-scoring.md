# Pro-Only Industry Scoring Report
**Date:** 2026-03-19
**Framework:** Revenue Optimization Framework (competitive-intel.md)
**Scoring:** WTP 30% + Frequency 25% + Pain 20% + Market 15% + SEO 10%

## Scoring Criteria for Pro Lock

An industry should be Pro-only if:
1. Weighted score >= 8.0 (high professional value)
2. Target users are professionals who'd pay $12/mo without hesitation
3. Agents serve specialized roles (not general consumer tasks)
4. Locking creates FOMO — free users see the tab and want access

An industry should stay FREE if:
1. It serves as a showcase/gateway (General)
2. Target audience is consumer/low-WTP (students, parents, hobbyists)
3. It drives top-of-funnel traffic that converts to Pro via other paths
4. Locking it would make the free tier feel too restrictive

## Final Selection: 9 Pro-Only Industries

1. **AI Agent Development** (9.35) — Already confirmed
2. **Cybersecurity** (9.15) — SOC analysts, pentesters, threat modelers
3. **Consulting & Strategy** (9.10) — Consultants, strategists, M&A
4. **Architecture & Engineering** (8.35) — AEC professionals, estimators
5. **SaaS & Product** (8.35) — Product managers, researchers, analysts
6. **Banking & Lending** (8.25) — Lenders, portfolio managers, KYC
7. **Pharma & Biotech** (8.20) — Regulatory, clinical trials, pharmacovigilance
8. **Aviation & Aerospace** (8.05) — Flight ops, MRO, safety, certification
9. **Executive & Leadership** (8.00) — C-suite, board prep, executive comms

## Rationale

9 Pro-only industries locks ~22% of all agents behind the paywall while keeping 31 industries free. This creates:
- **Generous free tier:** 31 industries with 3 generates. Users can explore widely.
- **Clear upgrade path:** 9 premium industries visible but blurred. FOMO is immediate.
- **Professional targeting:** Every locked industry targets users billing $100+/hr. $12/mo is trivial.
- **Balanced feel:** ~78% of content is free. This doesn't feel restrictive — it feels like there's a premium tier worth having.
